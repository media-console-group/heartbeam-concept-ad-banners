How It Works

  • The patient app guides the recording, and the patient presses the recorder to the chest.
  • The recording goes to the HeartBeam cloud, which synthesizes a 12-lead ECG. A physician reviews it, per the company.
  • “Similar, but not identical” to a standard 12-lead ECG
  • Cleared for arrhythmia assessment only
